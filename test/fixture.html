Why cats sleep so much – and why it’s fine

By Jane Doe

Cats sleep for between twelve and sixteen hours a day. That is a lot more than most mammals, and it has a good reason behind it.

In the wild, cats are hunters. Hunting takes a great deal of energy, so the body saves power between meals by resting & dozing.1

The science

Researchers found that cats spend about three quarters of their sleep in light sleep. They can wake quickly if they hear a threat nearby.
